# DocSend Setup Guide for Pitch Deck Tracking

## Overview
DocSend is a secure document sharing platform that provides detailed analytics on who views your pitch deck and how they engage with it. This guide will help you set up your account and optimize it for tracking investor engagement.

---

## Part 1: Account Creation

### Step 1: Sign Up for DocSend
1. Go to [DocSend.com](https://www.docsend.com)
2. Click **"Get Started Free"** or **"Sign Up"**
3. Choose your plan:

**Recommended Plan: Personal ($15/month)**
- 100 document visits per month
- Document analytics
- Email capture
- NDA and email verification
- Password protection
- Perfect for early-stage fundraising with targeted outreach

**Upgrade Option: Standard ($65/month)**
- Unlimited visits
- Team collaboration features
- Custom branding
- Advanced permissions
- Consider if you're sending to 30+ investors monthly

4. Enter your information:
   - Email address (use your company email)
   - Password
   - Company name

5. Complete email verification

### Step 2: Connect Your Cloud Storage (Optional but Recommended)
DocSend integrates with major cloud providers for seamless file management:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations**
2. Connect your preferred service:
   - **Dropbox** (recommended - DocSend is owned by Dropbox)
   - Google Drive
   - Microsoft OneDrive
   - Box

**Benefits:**
- Upload files directly from cloud storage
- Automatic syncing if you update the pitch deck
- Faster access to your document library

## Part 2: Upload and Configure Your Pitch Deck

### Step 3: Upload Your Pitch Deck
1. Click **"Upload"** or **"New Document"**
2. Choose upload method:
   - **From Computer:** Browse and select your PDF
   - **From Cloud Storage:** Navigate your connected drive

**File Requirements:**
- **Format:** PDF (strongly recommended for consistent viewing)
- **Size:** Under 10MB (optimize images if larger)
- **Length:** Under 20 slides for optimal investor engagement
- **File name:** Use clear naming: "CompanyName_PitchDeck_[Month][Year].pdf"

3. Wait for upload to complete (typically 10-30 seconds)

### Step 4: Configure Document Settings
After upload, configure your document:

1. **Document Name (Internal):**
   - Example: "Daphne AI Startup - Pitch Deck Q1 2026"
   - This is for your reference only

2. **Document Description (Optional):**
   - Add notes about this version
   - Example: "Pre-seed pitch deck v3.2 - Updated with Q4 metrics"

3. Click **"Save"**

## Part 3: Create Trackable Links for Investors

### Step 5: Create Investor-Specific Links
**CRITICAL BEST PRACTICE:** Create a unique link for each investor. Never reuse links.

#### For Each Investor:
1. Open your pitch deck in DocSend
2. Click **"Create Link"** or **"Share"**
3. Configure link settings:

**Link Settings:**

**A. Account/Contact Name** (Required)
- Enter the investor or firm name
- Example: "John Smith - AI Ventures"
- This helps you identify who viewed the deck in analytics

**B. Security Settings:**
Choose appropriate protection:

- **Email Capture (Recommended):**
  - ✅ Turn ON "Require email to view"
  - Ensures you know exactly who accessed the deck
  - Captures email for follow-up

- **Password Protection (Optional):**
  - Add password for highly sensitive decks
  - Share password separately (via text or call)
  - Note: Can create friction; use sparingly

- **NDA Requirement (Advanced):**
  - ✅ Require viewers to agree to NDA before viewing
  - Recommended for decks with proprietary information
  - DocSend provides a standard NDA template

- **Link Expiration (Recommended):**
  - Set expiration date (e.g., 30-90 days)
  - Example: Expire after fundraising round closes
  - Prevents outdated deck circulation

**C. Download Settings:**
- **Disable downloads** (Recommended): Forces viewing through DocSend for tracking
- **Enable downloads:** Use only for final-stage investors or if requested

**D. Notification Preferences:**
- ✅ Email me when document is viewed
- ✅ Email me when document viewing is completed
- ✅ Real-time notifications (optional - can be overwhelming)

### Step 6: Generate and Copy Link
1. Click **"Create Link"**
2. Copy the unique URL (looks like: `docsend.com/view/xxxxx`)
3. Paste into a spreadsheet to track which link goes to which investor

**Link Management Template:**
Create a tracking spreadsheet with columns:
- Investor Name
- Firm
- DocSend Link
- Date Sent
- Last Viewed
- Status (Sent, Viewed, Follow-up, Passed)

## Part 4: Send Your Pitch Deck to Investors

### Step 7: Install Email Plugin (Optional but Recommended)
For seamless sending and tracking:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Email Integrations**
2. Install plugin for your email:
   - **Gmail Plugin:** Install from Chrome Web Store
   - **Outlook Plugin:** Install from Microsoft Store

**Benefits:**
- Send DocSend links directly from your email client
- Automatic tracking without leaving your inbox
- Easy access to analytics

### Step 8: Compose Your Investor Email
Use this structure:

**Email Template:**
```
Subject: [Specific, compelling subject line]

Hi [Investor Name],

[Personalized 1-2 sentence opener]

I'm sharing our pitch deck for [Company Name], where we're building [specific
AI technology] for [target market]. We've achieved [key traction metric] and
are raising [amount] to [specific goal].

View our deck here: [DocSend Link]

[Brief context on why this investor specifically - their portfolio fit, thesis
alignment, etc.]

I'd love to discuss how [your unique value prop] addresses [problem they care
about]. Are you available for a 15-minute call next week?

Best,
[Your Name]
[Title]
[Company Name]
[Phone]
```

**Pro Tips:**
- Send emails Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10am or 2-4pm PST for best open rates
- Keep email body short - let the deck do the talking
- Personalize each email - no mass sends
- Follow up 3-5 days after they view the deck

## Part 5: Track and Analyze Investor Engagement

### Step 9: Monitor Your DocSend Analytics
DocSend provides powerful engagement insights:

#### **Dashboard Overview:**
Access analytics at **Home** > **Documents** > [Click your pitch deck]

**Key Metrics to Track:**

1. **View Rate:**
   - How many investors opened your link
   - Target: 60-80% view rate
   - Low rate? Improve your email subject line

2. **Time Spent:**
   - Total time each investor spent on your deck
   - Average time per investor
   - Benchmark: 3-4 minutes is engaged viewing
   - Under 1 minute = quick skim (needs follow-up)
   - Over 8 minutes = very interested (priority follow-up!)

3. **Page-by-Page Analytics:**
   - Which slides get the most attention
   - Slides that get skipped
   - **Insight:** If investors skip your team slide, they might want more product focus
   - **Insight:** Long time on financial projections = serious interest

4. **Completion Rate:**
   - Did they view the entire deck or drop off?
   - Drop-off points indicate potential issues
   - Example: If 50% drop at slide 8, that slide needs revision

5. **Return Visits:**
   - How many times the same investor revisited
   - Multiple visits = strong interest signal
   - **Action:** Reach out proactively after second view

#### **Real-Time Notifications:**
When an investor views your deck:
- You'll receive an email notification
- Check analytics to see their engagement depth
- Follow up within 24 hours if they spent 3+ minutes

### Step 10: Use Insights to Improve Your Pitch
DocSend analytics reveal patterns:

**Optimization Strategies:**

1. **If "Problem" slide has low engagement:**
   - Make the problem more concrete and painful
   - Add customer quotes or data

2. **If "Solution" slide gets skipped:**
   - Simplify technical explanation
   - Lead with benefits, not features

3. **If investors drop off halfway:**
   - Move your "traction" slide earlier
   - Front-load the most compelling information

4. **If "Team" slide gets high attention:**
   - Expand team backgrounds
   - Add advisor logos if relevant

5. **If "Ask" slide has repeat views:**
   - Investor is interested!
   - Prepare for detailed financial questions

## Part 6: Manage Your Fundraising Pipeline

### Step 11: Track Follow-Ups
Based on DocSend analytics, categorize investors:

**Priority Tiers:**

**🔥 Hot Leads** (Follow up within 24 hours)
- Viewed entire deck (100% completion)
- Spent 5+ minutes
- Viewed 2+ times
- **Action:** Send follow-up email, propose meeting time

**🟡 Warm Leads** (Follow up within 3-5 days)
- Viewed 75%+ of deck
- Spent 2-4 minutes
- **Action:** Casual check-in, offer to answer questions

**❄️ Cold Leads** (Follow up within 1 week)
- Viewed less than 50%
- Spent under 1 minute or didn't view at all
- **Action:** Send a different hook or add fresh traction update

**❌ Passed**
- Explicitly declined
- No engagement after 2 follow-ups
- **Action:** Note feedback, move on, circle back in 6 months with progress

### Step 12: Update Your Deck Based on Feedback
As you collect investor feedback:

1. Create a new version in DocSend
2. **Important:** Don't replace the existing file
3. Upload as new document: "PitchDeck_v3.3"
4. Create new links for this version
5. Compare analytics across versions to see improvements

**Version Control Best Practices:**
- Keep each version in DocSend for historical tracking
- Label clearly: v1.0, v1.1, v2.0, etc.
- Note what changed: "Updated with Q1 revenue metrics"
- Track which version performed best

## Part 7: Advanced Features and Best Practices

### DocSend Best Practices for Fundraising:

1. **One Link Per Investor - Always**
   - Never reuse links
   - Ensures accurate attribution
   - Prevents link sharing to unintended parties

2. **Enable Email Capture**
   - Verify who actually viewed it
   - Useful if investors forward your deck internally

3. **Set Link Expiration**
   - 60-90 days for active fundraising
   - Shows urgency and professionalism

4. **Disable Downloads (Initially)**
   - Forces viewing through DocSend
   - Maintains tracking
   - Enable later for serious investors who request it

5. **Monitor Weekly**
   - Check analytics every Monday
   - Identify who needs follow-up
   - Spot deck improvement opportunities

6. **A/B Test Your Deck**
   - Create two versions with different approaches
   - Send each to 10 investors
   - Compare metrics to see what works

### Privacy and Security Settings:

**For Pre-Seed AI Startups:**
- ✅ Email verification (always)
- ✅ Link expiration (60-90 days)
- ⚠️ Password protection (only for highly proprietary tech)
- ⚠️ NDA requirement (use if discussing patents or trade secrets)
- ❌ Watermarks (appears unprofessional at pre-seed stage)

**IP Protection:**
- Don't include proprietary code or algorithms in pitch deck
- Save technical deep-dives for later-stage meetings
- Use DocSend's NDA feature for detailed technical appendix

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Reusing the same link** - Makes analytics useless
2. **Not following up on high-engagement leads** - Strike while interest is hot
3. **Ignoring drop-off patterns** - Deck needs iteration if 50%+ drop off early
4. **Overwhelming with notifications** - Check analytics weekly, not hourly
5. **Forgetting to update deck** - Stale metrics lose credibility
6. **Enabling downloads too early** - Lose tracking visibility

## Analytics Interpretation Guide

### What Good Engagement Looks Like:
- **View Rate:** 70%+ of sent links are opened
- **Avg Time Spent:** 3-5 minutes
- **Completion Rate:** 80%+ view entire deck
- **Return Visits:** 10-20% view deck 2+ times

### Red Flags:
- **View Rate under 40%:** Improve email subject line or sender reputation
- **Avg Time under 1 minute:** Deck opening isn't hooking investors
- **High drop-off on slide 3-5:** Problem/solution not compelling enough
- **Zero return visits:** Consider if messaging aligns with investor thesis
