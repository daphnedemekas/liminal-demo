# OpenVC Setup Guide for Daphne's AI Startup

## Overview
OpenVC is a free fundraising platform that connects startups with 20,000+ verified investors. This guide will walk you through setting up your account to target pre-seed and angel investors in the San Francisco Bay Area who focus on AI startups.

---

## Part 1: Account Creation

### Step 1: Sign Up
1. Go to [OpenVC.app](https://www.openvc.app)
2. Click **"Sign up"**
3. Select **"I'm a founder"**
4. Fill in your personal information:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Password
5. Click **"Next"**

### Step 2: Validate Your Email
1. Check your email inbox for a validation link from OpenVC
2. Click the link to activate your account
3. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

---

## Part 2: Complete Your Company Profile

### Step 3: Set Up Company Information
When prompted (or via Settings > Company Profile), enter:

**Basic Information:**
- **Company Name:** [Your AI startup name]
- **Website:** [Your company website URL]
- **Industry:** Select **"Artificial Intelligence"** or **"AI/ML"**
- **Stage:** Select **"Pre-seed"** or **"Angel"**
- **Location:** San Francisco, CA

**Company Description:**
Write a compelling 2-3 sentence description emphasizing:
- What your AI technology does
- The problem it solves
- Your unique approach (technology mediation layer focus)

**Example:**
"We're building an AI-powered technology mediation layer that [specific value proposition]. Our solution enables [target users] to [key benefit], addressing a $X billion market opportunity in [sector]."

**Additional Details:**
- **Founded:** [Year]
- **Team Size:** [Number]
- **Funding Goal:** [Amount you're raising]
- **Current Revenue/Traction:** [Key metrics]

---

## Part 3: Complete the Onboarding Checklist

### Step 4: Take the Fundability Test
This is OpenVC's 30-question assessment that evaluates your startup readiness:

1. Navigate to the **Fundability Test** in your dashboard
2. Answer all 30 questions honestly about:
   - Your team
   - Product/market fit
   - Traction and metrics
   - Business model
   - Competition
3. Review your results to identify any "red flags" to address before pitching

**Pro Tip:** Fix any red flags identified before reaching out to investors. This increases your success rate.

### Step 5: Upload Your Pitch Deck
1. Go to **Documents** or **Pitch Deck** section
2. Click **"Upload Pitch Deck"**
3. Upload your deck as a PDF (recommended format)
4. Ensure it's under 20 slides for optimal investor engagement

**Pitch Deck Best Practices:**
- Keep it visual and concise
- Focus on problem, solution, traction, team, and ask
- Tailor messaging for AI investors (technical depth + business impact)

### Step 6: Invite Co-founders (Optional)
If you have co-founders:
1. Go to **Teams** page
2. Click **"Invite team members"**
3. Enter their email addresses
4. They'll receive an invitation to join your OpenVC account

---

## Part 4: Find and Target AI Investors in SF Bay Area

### Step 7: Search for Relevant Investors
OpenVC has powerful filtering to find the right investors:

1. Go to **Investors** or **Search** section
2. Apply these filters:

**Primary Filters:**
- **Stage:** Pre-seed, Angel
- **Industry:** Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, SaaS (if applicable)
- **Location:** San Francisco, Bay Area, California, United States
- **Check Size:** [Your target range, e.g., $100K-$500K]

**Additional Filters to Consider:**
- **Previous investments:** Look for investors who've backed similar AI startups
- **Investment thesis:** Filter for those mentioning "AI infrastructure," "enterprise AI," or related themes
- **Active investors:** Prioritize investors who recently made investments (2024-2026)

### Step 8: Build Your Target List
OpenVC provides curated lists. Start with:
- [Top AI Investors & VC Firms](https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/ai-investors)
- [Pre-Seed Investors](https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/pre-seed-investors)
- [Angel Investors in the USA](https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/angel-investors-usa)

**Action Steps:**
1. Review each investor profile
2. Add promising investors to your **Pipeline**
3. Take notes on why each investor is a good fit
4. Aim for 50-100 qualified investors in your initial pipeline

**Key Investors to Research:**
For SF Bay Area AI pre-seed/angel investors, look for:
- AI-focused angels and micro-VCs
- Former AI company founders who now invest
- Firms with dedicated AI thesis
- Accelerators with SF presence (YC, etc.)

---

## Part 5: Personalize and Send Outreach

### Step 9: Craft Personalized Emails
OpenVC offers AI-powered email personalization:

1. Select an investor from your pipeline
2. Click **"Send Pitch"** or **"Email"**
3. Use OpenVC's AI to personalize the message based on:
   - Investor's previous investments
   - Their stated investment thesis
   - Common connections or interests

**Email Template Structure:**
```
Subject: [Compelling, specific hook about your AI solution]

Hi [Investor Name],

[1-2 sentence personalized opener referencing their portfolio/interests]

We're [Company Name], building [specific AI technology] to [concrete problem].
We've [key traction metric] and are raising [amount] to [specific milestone].

[Investor Name] at [Portfolio Company] suggested we connect given your interest
in [specific AI area]. Our approach to [unique angle] aligns with your thesis
on [their stated interest].

Would you be open to a 15-minute call next week?

Best,
[Your Name]
[Title]
[Contact Info]
```

### Step 10: Track Your Pipeline
OpenVC automatically tracks:
- Who opened your deck
- Time spent on each page
- Follow-up status

**Pipeline Management:**
1. Move investors through stages: Contacted → Interested → Meeting → Due Diligence
2. Set reminders for follow-ups (3-5 days for warm leads)
3. Log all interactions and feedback

## Part 6: Optimize for Success

### Ongoing Actions:
- **Update metrics weekly:** Keep traction numbers current in your profile
- **Refresh your pitch deck:** Based on investor feedback, iterate on messaging
- **Monitor analytics:** See which deck slides get the most attention
- **Network actively:** Attend SF AI/startup events, make warm introductions
- **Respond quickly:** Reply to interested investors within 24 hours

### Premium Features (Optional - $99/month or $299/year):
- Unlimited deck reviews
- Fundraising masterclass videos
- Increased daily outreach capacity
- Priority support

**Recommendation:** Start with the free version. Upgrade to Premium if you're doing high-volume outreach (50+ investors/month) or want the educational content.

## Target Investor Criteria Summary

**Your Ideal OpenVC Investor Profile:**
- **Stage:** Pre-seed, Angel
- **Industry Focus:** AI/ML, Enterprise SaaS, Developer Tools
- **Geography:** San Francisco Bay Area
- **Check Size:** $50K - $500K (adjust to your raise)
- **Investment Thesis:** Early-stage AI infrastructure, technology mediation, or similar
- **Portfolio Signals:** Previously invested in AI startups that reached Series A+

**Red Flags to Avoid:**
- Investors focused only on B2C or mobile apps
- Funds that haven't invested in AI in 2+ years
- Those requiring extensive traction for pre-seed (premature)
- Investors outside your geographic focus (unless remote-first thesis)
