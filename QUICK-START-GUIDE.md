## 🎯 Hour 1: Setup Calendly + OpenVC

### Step 1: Calendly (15 minutes)
**Goal**: Create scheduling link for OpenVC profile

1. Go to https://calendly.com/signup
2. Sign up with your email
3. Connect your Google Calendar
4. Create "Investor Meeting" event:
   - Duration: 30 minutes
   - Location: Zoom (auto-generated)
   - Availability: Weekdays 9am-5pm (adjust as needed)
5. Copy your Calendly link (looks like: `calendly.com/yourname/investor-meeting`)

✅ **Checkpoint**: Test your link in an incognito window

**📄 Detailed guide**: See `calendly-setup-checklist.md`

---

### Step 2: OpenVC (30 minutes)
**Goal**: Complete profile so investors can discover you

1. Go to https://www.openvc.app/
2. Click "Start Raising" or "Sign Up"
3. **Profile Information**:
   - Your name and email
   - Company name
   - Website (or leave blank if pre-launch)
   - LinkedIn Company Page (optional, can skip)
   - **Scheduling link**: Paste your Calendly link here

4. **Upload Pitch Deck**:
   - PDF format
   - Make sure AI technology is clearly highlighted

5. **Company Details**:
   - **Industry**: "Artificial Intelligence" or "Machine Learning"
   - **Sub-sector**: "AI Infrastructure" or "Developer Tools"
   - **Location**: San Francisco, CA
   - **Stage**: Pre-Seed
   - **Raising**: Enter your target amount (e.g., $500K-$1M)
   - **One-liner**: Your best 1-sentence pitch
     - ✅ Good: "AI mediation layer enabling LLM integration for enterprise apps"
     - ❌ Bad: "We use AI to help companies with their data"

6. **Team Information**:
   - Add all founders
   - LinkedIn profiles for each
   - Brief relevant experience

7. **Ideal Investor Profile**:
   - ✓ Pre-seed stage
   - ✓ AI/ML focus
   - ✓ San Francisco Bay Area
   - ✓ Check sizes: $50K-$500K
   - ✓ Angel investors and micro VCs

8. Complete OpenVC's onboarding checklist

✅ **Checkpoint**: Your OpenVC profile should be 100% complete

**📄 Detailed guide**: See `openvc-setup-guide.md`

## 🎯 Hour 2: DocSend + First Outreach

### Step 3: DocSend (45 minutes)
**Goal**: Set up tracked pitch deck links

1. Go to https://www.docsend.com
2. Click "Start Free Trial"
3. Choose **Standard Plan** trial ($45/month after - recommended)
   - Why: Personal plan has 100-visit limit that can break mid-fundraise
4. Enter your business email and company info

5. **Upload Your Pitch Deck**:
   - Click "+ New" → "Upload Document"
   - Upload your pitch deck PDF
   - Name it: `[CompanyName]-PitchDeck-Feb2026.pdf`

6. **Configure Settings** (CRITICAL):
   - ✓ Enable "Email required to view" (captures investor contact)
   - ✓ Enable "Email me when viewed" (instant notifications)
   - ✓ Enable "Email me when shared" (see who they forward to)
   - ✓ Disable "Allow downloading" (prevents uncontrolled sharing)
   - Add your logo
   - Customize link: `docsend.com/view/[yourcompany]-deck`

7. **Create 3 Link Versions**:
   - **Link 1**: `[company]-warm` (for warm intros)
   - **Link 2**: `[company]-cold` (for cold outreach)
   - **Link 3**: `[company]-vip` (for top-tier investors, add password)

   Why: Track which source gets best engagement

8. **Test Your Links**:
   - Open in incognito mode
   - Enter a test email
   - Verify you receive notification
   - Check analytics dashboard

✅ **Checkpoint**: You should have 3 working DocSend links

**📄 Detailed guide**: See `docsend-setup-guide.md`

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "Investors aren't responding to cold emails"
- Check: Is your subject line compelling?
- Fix: Try A/B testing different subject lines
- Alternative: Focus on warm intros (10x better response rate)

### "DocSend views are low (< 2 minutes)"
- Check: Is your deck too long? (aim for 12-15 slides)
- Fix: Simplify, lead with strongest traction
- Test: Send to advisor for feedback

### "I don't have warm intros"
- Strategy: Start with angel investors on Twitter/LinkedIn
- Action: Engage with their content for 2 weeks
- Request: Coffee meeting (not a pitch) to learn from them
- Result: Natural relationship → future intro

### "OpenVC activation email not arriving"
- Contact: steph@openvc.app
- Usually resolved within 24 hours

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip 1: Timing Matters
- **Best time to email VCs**: Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10am PT
- **Worst time**: Monday morning, Friday afternoon, weekends

### Tip 2: Quality > Quantity
- 10 well-researched, personalized emails > 50 generic blasts
- One warm intro > 20 cold emails

### Tip 3: Follow Up
- If no response after 1 week: follow up once
- If still no response: move on, try again in 3 months with traction update

### Tip 4: Track Everything
- Update your spreadsheet IMMEDIATELY after every interaction
- You'll forget details within 24 hours

### Tip 5: Momentum is Real
- Once you have one committed investor, others follow faster
- Lead with your strongest proof points (traction, team, market timing)
