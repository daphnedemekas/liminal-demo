# Quick Start Guide: OpenVC + DocSend Setup
## Get fundraising in 2 hours

---

## Your Goal
Set up OpenVC and DocSend so you can start sending tracked pitch decks to pre-seed/angel AI investors in San Francisco today.

---

## What You Need Before Starting

✅ **Pitch deck** (PDF, 15-18 slides, under 10MB)
✅ **Company email address** (not personal Gmail)
✅ **2 hours of focused time**
✅ **Credit card** for DocSend ($15/month)
✅ **Target raise amount** (e.g., $500K)

---

## Phase 1: OpenVC Setup (45 minutes)

### Step 1: Create Account (10 min)
1. Go to **[OpenVC.app](https://www.openvc.app)**
2. Click **"Sign up"** → **"I'm a founder"**
3. Enter:
   - Your name and email
   - Password
4. Verify email (check inbox)

### Step 2: Complete Profile (15 min)
1. **Personal Info:**
   - Upload professional headshot
   - Add LinkedIn URL

2. **Company Info:**
   - Company name
   - Website URL
   - **Industry:** Artificial Intelligence / AI/ML
   - **Stage:** Pre-seed or Angel
   - **Location:** San Francisco, CA
   - **Funding goal:** [Amount you're raising]

3. **Company Description:**
   Write 2-3 compelling sentences:
   ```
   [Company Name] builds [specific AI technology] for [target market].
   We've achieved [key traction metric] and are solving [specific problem]
   for [customer type]. Our unique approach to [differentiator] enables
   [key benefit].
   ```

### Step 3: Upload Pitch Deck (5 min)
1. Navigate to **Documents** or **Pitch Deck** section
2. Click **"Upload"**
3. Select your PDF deck
4. Wait for upload to complete

### Step 4: Take Fundability Test (15 min)
1. Go to **Fundability Test** in dashboard
2. Answer 30 questions honestly:
   - Team background
   - Traction and metrics
   - Market and competition
   - Business model
3. Review your score and identified "red flags"
4. **Note:** Address any critical red flags before pitching

✅ **OpenVC is now set up!**

---

## Phase 2: DocSend Setup (30 minutes)

### Step 1: Create Account (5 min)
1. Go to **[DocSend.com](https://www.docsend.com)**
2. Click **"Get Started"**
3. Choose **Personal plan** ($15/month)
4. Enter email, password, company name
5. Add payment info
6. Verify email

### Step 2: Connect Cloud Storage (5 min) [OPTIONAL]
1. Go to **Settings** → **Integrations**
2. Connect **Dropbox** or **Google Drive**
   - Makes uploading faster
   - Auto-syncs updates
3. Authorize the connection

### Step 3: Install Email Plugin (5 min) [OPTIONAL BUT RECOMMENDED]
1. Go to **Settings** → **Email Integrations**
2. Install plugin for:
   - **Gmail:** Chrome Web Store → "DocSend for Gmail"
   - **Outlook:** Microsoft Store → "DocSend for Outlook"
3. Grant permissions

### Step 4: Upload Pitch Deck (5 min)
1. Click **"Upload"** in main dashboard
2. Choose your pitch deck PDF:
   - From computer
   - OR from connected cloud storage
3. Name it clearly: **"CompanyName_PitchDeck_Q1_2026"**
4. Add description (optional): "Pre-seed pitch deck v1.0"
5. Click **"Save"**

### Step 5: Create First Investor Link (10 min)
1. Open your uploaded pitch deck
2. Click **"Create Link"** or **"Share"**
3. Configure settings:

**Link Name:**
- Enter: "[Investor Name] - [Firm Name]"
- Example: "Jane Smith - AI Ventures"

**Security Settings:**
- ✅ **Require email to view** (TURN ON)
- ❌ **Password protect** (leave OFF for now)
- ❌ **Require NDA** (leave OFF for initial outreach)
- ✅ **Set expiration date:** 60 days from today
- ❌ **Allow downloads** (TURN OFF - forces viewing through DocSend)

**Notifications:**
- ✅ Email me when document is viewed
- ✅ Email me when viewing is complete

4. Click **"Create Link"**
5. **Copy the link** (looks like: `docsend.com/view/abc123`)
6. Paste it somewhere safe (you'll need it for outreach)

✅ **DocSend is now set up!**

## Phase 3: Find Your First 10 Investors (30 minutes)

### Step 1: Access OpenVC Investor Lists (5 min)
Go to these curated lists:
1. **[AI Investors](https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/ai-investors)**
2. **[Pre-Seed Investors](https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/pre-seed-investors)**
3. **[Angel Investors USA](https://www.openvc.app/investor-lists/angel-investors-usa)**

### Step 2: Filter for Your Target Investors (10 min)
Use OpenVC search with these filters:
- **Stage:** Pre-seed, Angel
- **Industry:** Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning
- **Location:** San Francisco, Bay Area, California
- **Check Size:** [Your range, e.g., $100K-$500K]

### Step 3: Research Top 10 Prospects (15 min)
For each investor, check:
1. **Recent investments** (last 12 months) - look for AI startups
2. **Portfolio fit** - Do they have similar companies?
3. **LinkedIn profile** - Any mutual connections?
4. **Investment thesis** (from their website or blog)

**Add to a spreadsheet:**
- Investor Name
- Firm
- Email
- Why they're a good fit
- Personalization angle (portfolio company, thesis, etc.)

✅ **You now have your first 10 target investors!**

## Phase 4: Send Your First Outreach Email (15 minutes)

### Step 1: Create Unique DocSend Links (5 min)
For each of your 10 investors:
1. Go to DocSend → Your pitch deck
2. Click **"Create Link"**
3. Name it: "[Investor Name] - [Firm]"
4. Use same security settings as before
5. Copy link and add to your spreadsheet

**CRITICAL:** Never reuse the same link. Each investor gets their own.

### Step 2: Draft Personalized Email (10 min)

**Use this template:**

```
Subject: [Specific hook - their portfolio company or thesis area]

Hi [First Name],

I'm [Your Name], founder of [Company Name]. We're building [one-sentence
description of your AI solution] for [target customer].

I noticed you invested in [Portfolio Company], which aligns closely with
our approach to [specific problem area]. We've [key traction metric -
e.g., "signed 5 pilot customers" or "achieved $10K MRR"] and are currently
raising [amount] to [specific milestone - e.g., "scale to 50 customers"].

Would you be open to a 15-minute call to discuss? Here's our deck:
[DocSend Link]

Best,
[Your Name]
[Title]
[Company Name]
[Email] | [Phone]
```

**Personalization is KEY:**
- Reference a specific portfolio company
- Mention their investment thesis
- Show you've done research

### Step 3: Send to First 3 Investors (Test Batch)
Start small:
1. Pick your **3 warmest leads** (ideally with mutual connections)
2. Personalize each email
3. Send!
4. Wait 24-48 hours and monitor DocSend analytics

✅ **You've sent your first outreach emails!**

## What to Do Next (Ongoing)

### Daily (10 minutes)
1. **Check DocSend analytics:**
   - Who viewed your deck?
   - How long did they spend?
   - Did they view the entire deck?
2. **Follow up with high-engagement leads:**
   - If someone spent 3+ minutes → Email them within 24 hours
   - If someone viewed 2+ times → They're interested, call them!

### Every 2-3 Days
1. **Send 3-5 new outreach emails** (scale gradually)
2. **Create unique DocSend link for each investor**
3. **Track all activity in your spreadsheet**

### Weekly
1. **Review analytics patterns:**
   - Which deck slides get the most attention?
   - Where do investors drop off?
   - Improve those sections
2. **Send follow-ups to non-responders** (after 5-7 days)
3. **Add 10-20 new investors to your target list**

---

## Quick Troubleshooting

### "Investors aren't viewing my deck"
- **Check:** Email open rates (use email tracking tool)
- **Fix:** Improve subject line - make it more specific and compelling
- **Try:** Reach out via LinkedIn message as backup

### "Investors view deck but don't respond"
- **Check:** DocSend time spent. If under 2 minutes, they skimmed.
- **Fix:** Improve first 3 slides - they're not hooking attention
- **Try:** More direct follow-up: "Saw you viewed the deck - what questions can I answer?"

### "My DocSend analytics are confusing"
- **High time spent (5+ min) + full completion = Hot lead** → Follow up ASAP
- **Multiple views = Very interested** → Proactive call
- **Under 1 min view = Skim** → Not interested or bad timing
- **No view after 5 days** → Send follow-up email with new angle

## Most Common Mistakes (AVOID THESE)

❌ **Reusing DocSend links** → Your analytics will be useless
❌ **Generic copy-paste emails** → Investors ignore these
❌ **Not following up** → Most deals happen after 2-3 touches
❌ **Sending to too many investors at once** → Can't personalize effectively
❌ **Giving up after rejections** → 90%+ rejection rate is NORMAL
❌ **Forgetting to update deck** → Stale metrics lose credibility
❌ **No tracking system** → You'll lose track of conversations
