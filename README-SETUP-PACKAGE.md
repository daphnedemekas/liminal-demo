## 🆘 Common Questions

### "Do I really need all these tools?"
**Yes.**
- OpenVC: Free access to 20K+ investors + credibility
- DocSend: Know who's interested before they tell you
- Calendly: Save 10+ hours of email back-and-forth
- Tracking: Stay organized through 50+ conversations

### "Can I use free alternatives?"
**Partially.**
- OpenVC: Free forever (90% of features)
- Calendly: Free tier works fine
- DocSend: No good free alternative with same analytics
- Tracking: Google Sheets is free

**Total cost**: $45/month for DocSend (worth it)

### "How long does fundraising take?"
**Realistic timeline**: 8-12 weeks from first contact to closed round
- Week 1-2: Setup + initial outreach
- Week 3-6: Active pitching
- Week 7-10: Closing
- Week 11-12: Buffer for delays

### "What if I don't have warm intros?"
**Start building relationships**:
1. Identify angel investors on Twitter/LinkedIn
2. Engage with their content for 2 weeks
3. Request coffee (not a pitch) to learn
4. Natural relationship → future warm intro
5. One warm intro beats 20 cold emails

### "What if investors aren't responding?"
**Diagnose the issue**:
- <20% response rate? → Refine targeting or subject lines
- Low DocSend views? → Deck may be too long/unclear
- Meetings but no follow-ups? → Pitch needs work
- See troubleshooting in MASTER-CHECKLIST.md
