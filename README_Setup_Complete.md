## 🎓 Key Concepts

### OpenVC
**What it is:** Free platform with 20,000+ verified investors
**What you use it for:**
- Finding pre-seed/angel investors who invest in AI startups
- Filtering by geography (SF Bay Area), stage, check size
- Researching investor portfolios and thesis
- Tracking your fundraising pipeline

**Cost:** Free (Premium: $99/month optional)

---

### DocSend
**What it is:** Secure document sharing with detailed analytics
**What you use it for:**
- Sharing pitch deck with unique tracking links per investor
- Seeing who viewed your deck, for how long, and which slides
- Identifying hot leads (high engagement) vs. cold leads
- Protecting your IP with email verification and expiration

**Cost:** $15/month (Personal plan sufficient for pre-seed)

---

### How They Work Together
1. **Find investors** on OpenVC (filter for AI, pre-seed, SF)
2. **Research** their portfolio and investment thesis
3. **Create unique DocSend link** for each investor
4. **Send personalized email** with DocSend link
5. **Monitor engagement** in DocSend analytics
6. **Follow up** based on viewing behavior
7. **Track everything** in your investor spreadsheet

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Do's:
✅ **Personalize every email** - Reference specific portfolio companies
✅ **Create unique DocSend link per investor** - Never reuse links
✅ **Follow up consistently** - Most deals happen after 2-3 touches
✅ **Track everything** - Use the investor tracking template religiously
✅ **Update metrics regularly** - Refresh your deck with latest traction monthly
✅ **Respond fast to hot leads** - If someone views deck 2+ times, call them!

### Don'ts:
❌ **Mass email blasts** - Generic emails get ignored
❌ **Reusing DocSend links** - Ruins your analytics
❌ **Forgetting to follow up** - Investors are busy, remind them
❌ **Giving up after rejections** - Fundraising is a volume game
❌ **Ignoring analytics** - DocSend tells you who's interested
❌ **Waiting too long** - Follow up within 24 hours of high engagement

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "I'm overwhelmed, where do I start?"
→ Open **QUICK_START_GUIDE.md** and do Phase 1 only today. Tomorrow, do Phase 2.

### "My pitch deck isn't ready"
→ Use **AI_Pitch_Deck_Template.md** to create or refine it. Don't wait for perfection - get v1.0 out and iterate based on investor feedback.

### "I don't know any investors"
→ That's exactly what OpenVC solves. Start with their curated lists (see OpenVC_Setup_Guide.md).

### "No one is responding to my emails"
→ Check QUICK_START_GUIDE troubleshooting section. Likely issues: subject line, personalization, or email deliverability.

### "Investors are viewing but not responding"
→ Check DocSend analytics. If time spent < 2 min, improve your first 3 slides. If time spent > 5 min, follow up more aggressively!

### "This feels like too much work"
→ Fundraising IS a full-time job for 2-3 months. But these tools automate much of the grunt work. Focus on personalization and follow-up.
