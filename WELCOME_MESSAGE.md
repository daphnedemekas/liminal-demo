Hello, and welcome to our demo! 

Liminal is your companion for long-term projects. Whether you're writing a novel, planning a garden, or building a business, we help you make progress and pick up where you left off—even weeks or months later.

**The Exploration Page**

When you start, you'll chat with the AI to explore what you want to work on. 
Together, you'll identify your project goals, and when you're ready, we'll create 
a project path to help you make progress.

**Your Project Path**

Once you've accepted a goal, the AI gets to know you better: your background, 
what you already know, and where you might need help. It then creates a custom 
roadmap tailored to you. When you accept that roadmap, your project branches into 
a series of focused deep dives.

**The Deep Dives**

During each exploration, the AI tracks your progress and understanding. If you 
get confused or show interest in something specific, the conversation adapts in 
real-time to help you out.

**Audio Feature**

All of the AI's responses come with audio narration, so you can listen along as you read. This makes it easier to absorb information and gives you a more natural conversation experience. You can control the audio playback in the chat interface.

**Multiple Projects**

You can always go back to the exploration page and brainstorm ideas for new projects. These 
can be about anything— creative work, business goals, hobbies, 
or personal growth. If the identified project doesn't seem quite right, you can 
always modify or negotiate, or create one yourself directly. 

**Your Project History**

Everything you explore gets tracked in the trajectory panel, so you can see how 
your understanding and progress develop over time. It's like a map of your project journey.

**Interactive Practice**

Throughout your project path, you'll encounter interactive exercises and artifacts that help you practice what you're working on. The are both fun to scroll through, productive for your goals, and they help the AI verify that you're actually understanding and remembering things. 


Thank you for taking the time to explore Liminal. We're excited to share our vision for adaptive, personalized project support and continue developing a platform that helps you make progress on the things that matter to you.
