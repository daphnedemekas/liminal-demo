Hello, and welcome to our demo! 

Liminal is an adaptive learning platform that creates personalized content and lesson plans just for you. We use advanced multi-agent AI systems that learn about you as you go, and actively refine your understanding.

**The Exploration Page**

So let's get in to how to use the protoype. When you start, you'll chat with the AI to figure out your learning style and what you want to learn. Together, you'll identify your goals, and when you're ready to commit to one, we'll create a learning path for you.

**Your Learning Path**

Once you've accepted a goal, the AI gets to know you better—your background, what you already know, and where you might be confused. It then creates a custom curriculum tailored to you. When you accept that curriculum, your path branches into a series of focused teaching sessions.

**The Teaching Sessions**

During each teaching session, the AI tracks how well you're understanding things. If you get confused or show interest in something specific, the teaching adapts in real-time to help you out.

**Audio Feature**

All of the AI's responses come with audio narration, so you can listen along as you read. This makes it easier to absorb information and gives you a more natural conversation experience. You can control the audio playback in the chat interface.

**Multiple Learning Paths**

You can always go back to the exploration page and start a new learning path for a different interest. These can be about anything, from your work goals, to hobbies, to your philosophical or creative ponderings. If the identified learning goal doesn’t seem quite right, you can always modify or negotiate to get the learning goal that feels right.  

**Your Learning Trajectory**

Everything you learn gets tracked in the learner trajectory panel, so you can see how your understanding develops over time. It's like a map of your learning journey.

**Interactive Practice**

Throughout your learning path, you'll encounter interactive exercises and artifacts that help you practice what you're learning. The are both fun to scroll through, productive for your goals, and they help the AI verify that you're actually understanding and remembering things. 


Thank you for taking the time to explore Liminal. We're excited to share our vision for adaptive, personalized learning and continue developing a platform that encourages your cognitive expansion.
