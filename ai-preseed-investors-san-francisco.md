## 🎯 Outreach Strategy by Type

### **For Accelerators (Heavybit, Alchemist, YC):**
- **Action**: Complete formal applications
- **Timeline**: Apply immediately (rolling admissions for most)
- **Prep**: Polish 2-minute demo video
- **Follow-up**: None needed, they'll contact you

### **For Micro VCs (Array, Banyan, Pioneer):**
- **Best Approach**: Warm intro from portfolio company founder or technical advisor
- **Alternative**: Cold email with compelling subject line: "AI mediation layer for [specific use case] - [key traction metric]"
- **Follow-up**: 1 week after initial email if no response

### **For Top-Tier Firms (Initialized, NFX, Conviction):**
- **Required**: Warm intro (don't cold email)
- **Strategy**: Build relationships with portfolio founders first
- **Timeline**: 2-3 months of relationship building
- **Entry Point**: Demo at portfolio company event or through advisor connection

### **For Angels:**
- **Best Approach**: Twitter/LinkedIn engagement first
- **Action**: Request 20-minute coffee meeting (not a pitch)
- **Goal**: Relationship building; mention fundraise casually
- **Follow-up**: Send deck only if they ask

## 💡 Pro Tips for Your AI Mediation Layer Pitch

### **What SF AI Investors Want to See:**
1. ✅ **Technical depth**: They're engineers - show the hard technical problems you've solved
2. ✅ **LLM-native approach**: Why now? What's possible that wasn't 12 months ago?
3. ✅ **Developer adoption**: Even small numbers (50 devs using it) > no users
4. ✅ **Enterprise pull**: Any signal that enterprises need this (letters of intent, design partnerships)
5. ✅ **Founder-market fit**: Have you built similar systems before?

### **Red Flags to Avoid:**
- ❌ "We'll be the AWS of AI" (too broad)
- ❌ No clear customer (who pays you?)
- ❌ Pure API wrapper without moat
- ❌ "Market size is $2T" (too generic)
- ❌ No technical differentiation

### **Traction Metrics They Care About:**
- Developer signups/waitlist
- GitHub stars (if open source)
- API calls per day
- Revenue (even $1K MRR is meaningful)
- Enterprise LOIs or design partnerships
- Technical blog traffic (shows thought leadership)
