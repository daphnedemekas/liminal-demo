# Calendly Setup Checklist for Investor Meetings
**Quick Setup for OpenVC Integration**

## Why You Need This
OpenVC asks for a scheduling link during signup. Calendly makes it easy for investors to book time with you without back-and-forth emails.

## Setup Steps (15 minutes)

### 1. Create Free Calendly Account
- Go to: https://calendly.com/signup
- Sign up with your startup email
- Choose Free plan (sufficient for fundraising)

### 2. Connect Your Calendar
- Settings → Calendar Connection
- Connect Google Calendar or Outlook
- Grant permissions to check availability

### 3. Create "Investor Meeting" Event Type
- Click "+ Create" → "Event Type"
- **Event Name**: "Investor Meeting"
- **Duration**: 30 minutes (recommended for first calls)
- **Location**:
  - Option 1: Zoom (Calendly generates link automatically)
  - Option 2: Google Meet
  - Option 3: Phone call
  - Option 4: In-person (if SF-based investor)

### 4. Configure Availability
- Set your working hours (e.g., 9am-5pm PT)
- Buffer time: 15 minutes between meetings
- Maximum events per day: 4-5 (prevent burnout)
- Date range: Allow scheduling 2 weeks out

### 5. Customize Booking Page
- Add your photo
- Bio: "Founder of [Company] - Building AI mediation layer for enterprise applications"
- **Important**: Add your startup's website URL

### 6. Set Up Confirmation Email
- Customize the confirmation email template
- Include:
  - Calendar invite with location
  - Brief agenda: "15-minute overview of [Company] + Q&A"
  - Your DocSend pitch deck link (optional)
  - LinkedIn profile link

### 7. Get Your Calendly Link
- Copy your personal Calendly link
- Format: `calendly.com/yourname/investor-meeting`
- This is what you'll paste into OpenVC

### 8. Test It
- Open link in incognito mode
- Book a test meeting with yourself
- Verify calendar invite arrives correctly
- Cancel test meeting

## For OpenVC Profile
When OpenVC asks for "Scheduling Link", paste:
```
https://calendly.com/yourname/investor-meeting
```

## Pro Tips
- ✅ Keep availability current (update if traveling)
- ✅ Set reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before
- ✅ Add custom question: "How did you hear about us?"
- ✅ Enable email reminder to yourself when someone books
- ⚠️ Don't overbook yourself - quality > quantity in fundraising

## Alternative Tools
- **Cal.com** (open source, free): https://cal.com
- **Vimcal** (power users): https://www.vimcal.com
- **Hubspot Meetings** (if using Hubspot CRM): Free with account

**Setup Time**: 15 minutes
**Cost**: Free
**Value**: Saves hours of email back-and-forth per investor
