# DocSend Account Setup & Configuration Guide
**For: AI Startup Pitch Deck Distribution & Analytics**

## Why DocSend for Fundraising
DocSend provides critical insights into how investors engage with your pitch materials:
- See exactly which pages investors view and for how long
- Track who forwards your deck (and to whom)
- Receive notifications when your deck is opened
- Control access with email verification and passwords
- Professional presentation without email attachments

## Pricing Recommendation

### Start with: **Standard Plan** ($45/user/month with annual billing)
**Why**: The Personal plan ($10/month) has a 100-visit monthly limit with NO warning, which can cause your deck to stop working mid-fundraise. For active fundraising, Standard provides:
- Unlimited document views
- Per-page analytics (crucial for pitch decks)
- Custom branding
- Email verification gates
- NDA acceptance features

**Budget**: $45/month × 3-4 months of active fundraising = ~$180-200 total

### Alternative: 14-Day Free Trial
Start with the Advanced Data Rooms trial to test all features, then downgrade to Standard after trial.

## Account Setup Process

### Step 1: Registration
1. **Go to https://www.docsend.com**
2. **Click "Start Free Trial" or "Sign Up"**
3. **Choose Standard Plan** (or start with Advanced trial)
4. **Enter your details:**
   - Business email (use your startup domain if you have one)
   - Company name
   - Role: Founder/CEO

### Step 2: Connect Cloud Storage (Optional but Recommended)
- Integrate with Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive
- Allows you to keep master copies in your existing workflow
- Updates automatically when you edit the source file

### Step 3: Upload Your Pitch Deck

1. **Click "+ New" → "Upload Document"**
2. **Upload your pitch deck PDF**
   - Ensure final version reviewed
   - File name: `[CompanyName]-PitchDeck-[Date].pdf`

3. **Configure Document Settings:**

#### Essential Settings for Investor Pitch Decks:

**Access Controls:**
- ✓ Enable "Email required to view"
- ✓ Enable "Passcode protection" (optional - use for specific investors)
- ✓ Enable "Disable downloading" (prevents uncontrolled distribution)
- ✓ Enable "Disable forwarding" or track forwarding
- ✓ Set expiration date (optional - typically 3-6 months)

**Analytics & Notifications:**
- ✓ Enable "Email me when viewed"
- ✓ Enable "Email me when shared"
- ✓ Track time spent per page
- ✓ Track visit frequency

**Branding:**
- Upload your logo
- Set brand colors
- Customize the link slug: `docsend.com/view/your-startup-name`

**NDA (Optional but Recommended):**
- Enable NDA acceptance before viewing
- Use DocSend's template or upload your own
- Digital signature capture

### Step 4: Create Investor-Specific Links

**Best Practice**: Create separate links for different investor groups

1. **Click on your pitch deck → "Share" → "Create New Link"**

2. **Create Links for:**
   - **Warm Intros**: `[company]-deck-warm` (less restrictive)
   - **Cold Outreach**: `[company]-deck-cold` (more tracking)
   - **Specific Investors**: `[company]-deck-[investor-name]` (maximum tracking)

3. **Benefits:**
   - See which source drives most engagement
   - Customize access controls per audience
   - Better attribution in your CRM/tracking

### Step 5: Set Up Analytics Dashboard

DocSend automatically tracks:
- **Visit metrics**: Total views, unique viewers, time spent
- **Page-by-page data**: Which slides get the most attention
- **Visitor information**: Email, location, device, viewing patterns
- **Engagement score**: Overall interest level

**Key Metrics to Watch:**
- **Pages viewed**: Are they seeing your entire deck?
- **Time on key slides**: Financial projections, team, traction
- **Repeat visits**: Sign of serious interest
- **Forwarding**: Who else is looking at your deck?

### Step 6: Email Integration Setup

**Gmail Extension:**
1. Install DocSend for Gmail from Chrome Web Store
2. Send DocSend links directly from Gmail
3. Track opens without leaving your inbox

**Outlook Extension:**
1. Install DocSend for Outlook
2. Same tracking capabilities in Outlook

## Security & Privacy Best Practices

### For Sensitive Information:
1. **Create a separate "Detailed Deck"** with financials
   - Share only after initial interest
   - Stronger access controls
   - Shorter expiration window

2. **Use NDAs** for:
   - Detailed financial projections
   - Customer lists
   - Proprietary technology details

3. **Enable Email Verification**:
   - Prevents public link sharing
   - Captures investor contact info
   - Controls access

4. **Regular Access Audits**:
   - Review who's accessed your deck monthly
   - Revoke links that haven't been used
   - Update expired passwords

### What NOT to Include in Your Deck:
- Source code or IP details
- Unreleased product features
- Unannounced partnerships
- Individual customer revenue data

## Analytics Interpretation Guide

### Strong Interest Signals:
- ✅ Viewed all pages (100% completion)
- ✅ Spent 5+ minutes total
- ✅ Returned 2+ times
- ✅ Forwarded to partners (check email domains)
- ✅ Spent extra time on financials/traction

### Medium Interest:
- ⚠️ Viewed 60-80% of pages
- ⚠️ Spent 2-4 minutes
- ⚠️ Single view only

### Low Interest:
- ❌ Viewed <50% of pages
- ❌ Spent <90 seconds
- ❌ Bounced from first page

**Action Items Based on Signals:**
- **Strong**: Follow up within 24 hours to schedule meeting
- **Medium**: Wait 3-5 days, send additional context/traction update
- **Low**: Add to long-term nurture list, follow up in 2-3 months

## Troubleshooting

**Problem**: Investors can't access the link
- Check if email verification is blocking legitimate domains
- Verify link hasn't expired
- Test link in incognito mode

**Problem**: Low view completion rates
- Your deck may be too long (aim for 12-15 slides max)
- Opening slides may not be compelling enough
- Consider A/B testing different versions

**Problem**: Many views but no responses
- Check if your contact info is clear on last slide
- Consider adding a specific CTA ("Reply to schedule a call")
- Follow up proactively - don't wait for inbound

## Cost Optimization Tips

1. **Use one account for multiple team members**: Share login during active fundraise

3. **Annual billing**: Save 30% vs monthly
