# OpenVC Account Setup Guide
**For: AI Mediation Layer Startup - Pre-Seed/Angel Fundraising**

## Why OpenVC for Your AI Startup
OpenVC provides free access to 20,000+ verified investors (VCs, angels, family offices) and has helped startups raise over $1 billion from top firms like YC, Sequoia, and Google Ventures.

## Account Setup Process

### Prerequisites Before Registration
1. **LinkedIn Company Page** (optional but recommended)
   - URL format: `https://www.linkedin.com/company/[your-company]`
   - If you don't have one yet, you can skip this during signup

2. **Scheduling Link** (optional but highly recommended)
   - Set up a Calendly account (free tier works): https://calendly.com
   - Alternative tools: Cal.com, Hubspot, Vimcal
   - This allows investors to book meetings directly with you

3. **Pitch Deck** (required)
   - Have your PDF ready to upload
   - Recommended: 10-15 slides covering problem, solution, market, traction, team, ask

### Step-by-Step Registration

1. **Go to https://www.openvc.app/**
2. **Click "Start Raising" or "Sign Up"**
3. **Complete the founder profile:**
   - Your name and email
   - Company name: [Your AI startup name]
   - Company website
   - LinkedIn Company Page (optional)
   - Scheduling link (Calendly URL)

4. **Upload your pitch deck**
   - PDF format recommended
   - Ensure it clearly highlights your AI technology

5. **Complete your company profile:**
   - **Industry**: Select "Artificial Intelligence" or "Machine Learning"
   - **Sub-sector**: "AI Infrastructure" or "Developer Tools" (for mediation layer)
   - **Location**: San Francisco, CA
   - **Stage**: Pre-Seed
   - **Funding Target**: Enter your raise amount (e.g., $500K-$1M for pre-seed)
   - **One-liner**: Craft a compelling 1-sentence description
     - Example: "AI mediation layer enabling seamless integration between LLMs and enterprise applications"

6. **Add team information:**
   - Founder names and roles
   - LinkedIn profiles
   - Brief bios highlighting relevant AI/tech experience

7. **Specify your ideal investor profile:**
   - ✓ Pre-seed stage
   - ✓ AI/ML focus
   - ✓ San Francisco Bay Area (but also open to remote)
   - ✓ Check sizes: $50K-$500K
   - ✓ Angel investors and micro VCs

### Complete Onboarding Checklist (30 minutes)
OpenVC provides a checklist - follow it to ensure your profile is investor-ready.
