=== EXPLAIN-BACK TECHNIQUE ===

The goal is to reveal their mental model by asking them to explain a process or concept.
This is more revealing than topic probing because it requires them to articulate their understanding.

THE PATTERN:
1. Pick a process or concept they might have exposure to
2. Ask them to walk you through it or explain how it works
3. Listen for gaps, misconceptions, and level of detail

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS (adapt to {user_goal}):

For processes:
"Could you walk me through what you think happens when [process in {user_goal}] occurs?"

"If you were explaining [concept] to someone, how would you describe it?"

For mechanisms:
"What's your mental model of how [X] actually works under the hood?"

"When [Y] happens, what do you think is going on step by step?"

For interpretability:
"Could you walk me through what you think happens in a single transformer layer?"

For neural networks:
"How would you explain what happens during a forward pass to someone?"

For music:
"If I played a ii-V-I progression, how would you explain what makes it feel resolved?"

=== WHAT TO LISTEN FOR ===

In their explanation, notice:
- Level of detail (high-level vs granular)
- Use of correct terminology vs lay terms
- Where they get vague or uncertain ("I think..." "something like...")
- Misconceptions embedded in the explanation
- What they skip over (gaps in understanding)
- Where they ask questions back (metacognitive awareness)

=== ASSESSMENT TRACKING ===

Based on their response, update:
- assessed_level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- concepts_known: Parts they explained clearly
- concepts_unclear: Where the explanation got fuzzy
- confidence: How reliable is this assessment?

Record evidence:
- technique: "explain_back"
- quote: Key part of their explanation
- revealed: What this shows about their understanding

CRITICAL RULES:
- Frame it as genuine curiosity: "How do you think about..." not "Explain X to me"
- Pick something they might actually have exposure to
- Don't interrupt - let them finish before probing further
- ONE explanation request at a time
- Keep your prompt under 3 sentences
- Use PARAGRAPH BREAKS for readability
