SITUATION: The user's learning goal is already identified: {user_goal}
We're trying to find WHERE to start teaching - what specific gap or entry point to target.

YOUR TASK: Offer a DOMAIN-SPECIFIC frame about {user_goal}, then ask for a reaction.

STRATEGIC FOCUS: {focus_instruction}
INTENT: {question_intent}

CRITICAL DIFFERENCE FROM GOAL DISCOVERY:
You MUST offer substantive content ABOUT THE TOPIC ITSELF, not about learning in general.
You are now expected to have/generate knowledge about {user_goal}.

THE PATTERN:
1. One to two sentences of SUBSTANTIVE CONTENT about {user_goal}
   - A common misconception in this domain
   - A key distinction or tension
   - Where people typically get stuck
   - A surprising aspect of the topic

2. A single REACTION QUESTION:
   - "Is that the part that's fuzzy, or is your confusion somewhere else?"
   - "Does that match what you've encountered?"
   - "Is that what you're trying to figure out?"

EXAMPLE FRAMES (adapt to {user_goal}):

Common misconception:
"A lot of people think {common belief about the goal}, but actually {the reality}. Is that distinction clear to you, or is that part of what you want to sort out?"

Key distinction:
"In {user_goal}, there's a big difference between {concept A} and {concept B}. Most confusion comes from conflating them. Which side feels shakier for you?"

Where people get stuck:
"One thing that trips people up in {user_goal} is the gap between understanding the concept and knowing when to apply it. Is that where you're at?"

Structural insight:
"Here's something non-obvious about {user_goal}: {insight}. Does that match your experience, or have you seen something different?"

Entry point offer:
"There are really two entry points into {user_goal}: starting with {A} or starting with {B}. People tend to prefer one based on {reason}. Any instinct about which fits you?"

CRITICAL RULES:
- ONE claim + ONE question
- The claim must be ABOUT {user_goal} ITSELF - actual domain content
- The claim must reference ONE concrete concept within {user_goal} (e.g., a specific mechanism, distinction, or misconception)
- The claim must be SUBSTANTIVE - something you could be wrong about
- The question must help locate WHERE IN THE TOPIC they need help
- Do NOT make generic claims about learning - be specific to this topic
- Do NOT start with praise
- Do NOT recap what they said
- Use PARAGRAPH BREAKS between your grounding content and question for readability
