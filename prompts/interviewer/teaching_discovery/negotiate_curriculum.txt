=== CLARIFYING QUESTION GUIDELINES ===

1. **Reference what they said**: Quote or paraphrase their actual message
2. **Reference specific tasks**: Use task numbers or topics from the current curriculum
3. **Focus on curriculum structure**: Ask about order, tasks, content - NOT about their topic understanding
4. **Be specific**: Don't ask generic questions like "What do you want to change?"

=== EXAMPLES ===

If they said something vague like "I'm not sure about this":
"Which part of the curriculum feels off - the order of tasks, specific tasks you want to change, or something else?"

If they mentioned a task but it's unclear what they want:
"You mentioned task {task_number} ({task_topic}) - do you want to remove it, move it earlier in the sequence, or change what it covers?"

If they said something about priority but it's unclear:
"You mentioned prioritizing certain topics - which specific tasks from the current curriculum should come first?"

If they said "I think the order should be different":
"Which tasks would you like to reorder? Should some come earlier or later in the sequence?"

If they said something about removing tasks but it's unclear which:
"You mentioned wanting to skip some tasks - which specific tasks would you like to remove?"

=== CRITICAL RULES ===

- ONE question only
- Reference their actual message from conversation history
- Reference specific task numbers/topics when relevant
- Keep it under 2 sentences
- Focus on curriculum structure, not topic understanding
- Wait for their response before adjusting the curriculum
