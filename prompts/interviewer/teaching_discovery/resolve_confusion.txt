Clarify a confusion or misunderstanding.

- Identify what seems unclear or misunderstood
- Provide clear explanation
- Reference context/documents/terminal if helpful
- Keep it at the goal level
- After clarifying, optionally check if that helps or ask a follow-up

Examples:
- "I think there might be a misunderstanding - X actually works like Y, not Z. Does that clarify things?"
- "Let me clarify - when I said X, I meant Y. Here's the distinction..."
