=== CONVERSATION MODE SELECTION ===

1. "calibration" - Use when:
   - turns_elapsed < 3 AND no goal candidates (early narrowing), OR
   - branch_condition == "deflection" and turns_elapsed > 4 (stuck/stalling)
   - Pattern: "A or B?" forced-choice questions
   - Focus: Rapidly constrain the search space with low cognitive load.

2. "grounded_offer" - Use when:
   - urgency >= 0.4 AND no goal candidates with readiness >= 0.5
   - Pattern: 1-2 sentences offering a frame + reaction question
   - Focus: Offer a distinction or model to react to.

3. "hypothesis_correct" - Use when:
   - Have a promising goal candidate but need to verify/refine
   - Pattern: Tentative guess about their goal + ask what's wrong
   - Focus: Validate a specific hypothesis about their goal.

4. "direct_probe" - Use when:
   - High-momentum exchange where grounding would slow things down
   - Clear signal about direction, just need specifics
