REASONS TO ADJUST:

1. PREREQUISITE GAP DETECTED
   - User is confused because they're missing foundation
   → INSERT prerequisite step before current step

2. MOVING TOO FAST
   - User showing confusion signals
   - Markers not advancing despite explanations
   → SPLIT current step into smaller pieces, or ADD review step

3. MOVING TOO SLOW
   - User demonstrating strong understanding quickly
   - Getting bored or impatient signals
   → SKIP ahead, MERGE steps, or ADD challenge

4. USER INTEREST SHIFT
   - User asking about related but different aspect
   - Opportunity to follow their curiosity
   → REORDER steps or ADD new relevant step

5. BETTER APPROACH DISCOVERED
   - Initial explanation approach isn't landing
   - User's questions suggest different angle would work better
   → MODIFY step's explanation_approach

WHEN NOT TO ADJUST:

- Normal learning struggle (productive confusion)
- Single confused moment (wait for pattern)
- Just started a new step (give it time)
- Already adjusted recently (avoid thrashing)

ADAPTATION TYPES:

1. INSERT_STEP: Add new step at position
2. REMOVE_STEP: Skip a step entirely
3. MODIFY_STEP: Change approach/content of existing step
4. REORDER: Change sequence of upcoming steps
5. SPLIT_STEP: Break one step into multiple
6. MERGE_STEPS: Combine steps that can be faster
7. NO_CHANGE: Keep plan as-is
